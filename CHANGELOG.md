# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Added article quick-preview dialog.
- Added publisher/source filter.
- Added persistent saved social search shortcuts.
- Added separate tabs for saved headlines and social searches.

## 0.2.0
- Added live Google News RSS fetching, regional/language/date filters, and saved headlines.

## 0.1.0
- Initial discovery UI and social platform search shortcuts.

## 0.4.0
- Added research desk with persistent notes.
- Added topic collections and ability to add search topics/headlines.
- Added JSON export of saved headlines, social searches, notes, and collections.

## 0.5.0
- Added edit/cancel for research notes.
- Added text search across research notes.
- Added JSON backup and restore for saved research data.
- Added a "Make research note" action from headline preview.

## 0.6.0
- Added unified research timeline for saved headlines, social searches, notes, and collection entries.
- Added timeline type filters and source-opening actions.
